Welcome to Nrusinha Tarun Mandal

Nrusinha Tarun Mandal is associated with the spirit of devotion, community participation, cultural activities, and togetherness. Our website shares information about our mandal, events, gallery, committee, and contact details.

We aim to celebrate our traditions and encourage people to participate in community activities with enthusiasm and respect.

Location: Chavan Galli, Kasaba Bawada, Kolhapur, Maharashtra 416006.
